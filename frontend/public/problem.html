Problem Statement

Understanding the challenges facing Horizon State University

The Core Challenge

No Early Warning System for At-Risk Students

Data Silos Create Blind Spots

Data resided in isolated departmental systems—Academic Affairs, Financial Aid, Housing, and Student Life—with no cross-functional integration or holistic student view. This fragmentation created three critical issues:

1. Data Accessibility Crisis

Advisors required 15–20 minutes per student to manually query multiple systems, delaying the identification of warning signs and coordination of support services.

  • Academic data locked in separate portals
  • Financial information siloed in aid systems
  • Housing and engagement data disconnected
  • Manual cross-referencing required for each student

2. Reactive Interventions

The university responded to manifest problems (e.g., academic probation after failing grades, financial counseling after payment defaults), missing critical opportunities for preventive action.

  • Students identified only after failing courses
  • Financial issues detected after payment defaults
  • Engagement problems recognized too late
  • Limited ability to coordinate early support services

3. Inconsistent Risk Assessment

Different departments used varying criteria, leading to a non-standardized methodology for evaluating combined risk factors and prioritizing limited intervention resources.

  • No unified definition of "at-risk"
  • Inconsistent thresholds across departments
  • Difficulty prioritizing intervention resources
  • Lack of evidence-based decision making

The Result: Below-Average Retention

These systemic issues contributed to HSU's below-average student retention rates, with advisors spending more time gathering data than actually advising students. The reactive approach meant intervention came too late for many at-risk students.

Stakeholder Impact

How data silos affect different university stakeholders

Stakeholder Pain Point Solution Requirement
Academic Advisors Time spent on manual data retrieval; difficulty tracking intervention outcomes. Student 360° Profile with unified data and persistent Advisor Notes for documentation.
Department Chairs Inability to compare program performance (e.g., Engineering vs. Psychology) or justify resource allocation with evidence. Comparative Department Performance charts and Deep Dive analytics.
Financial Aid Officers Disconnect between academic struggles and financial payment issues. Integrated Financial Card in 360° Profile to enable strategic aid targeting.
Institutional Leadership Need for high-level, real-time Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for accreditation and strategic planning. Dashboard View with retention rate and at-risk student count KPIs.
