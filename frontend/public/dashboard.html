Key Insights & Analysis

1. Department Performance Variation

The Department Performance Bar Chart reveals the Psychology department with the lowest average GPA compared to Engineering. This anomaly highlights an opportunity for targeted intervention, such as allocating dedicated academic support personnel or reviewing curriculum in the Psychology department.

Psychology
Lowest GPA Department → Priority for Support

2. Multi-Factor Correlation Evidence

The predictive model provides concrete evidence of multi-factor correlation. The system identified that 75% of students flagged with a Critical Risk Score exhibited both a GPA below 2.5 AND an Outstanding Balance above $15,000.

This correlation validates the dashboard's holistic design, confirming that academic and financial stress are deeply interconnected, requiring coordinated intervention.

75%
Critical Risk Students with Combined Academic & Financial Issues

3. Proactive Intervention Opportunity

The dashboard's identification of 291 at-risk students (29.1%) enables an immediate shift from a reactive to a proactive intervention model. Advisors are empowered to use the Pulse Tracker to rank and contact the highest-risk students first.

This ensures that limited university resources are directed to the cases most likely to benefit from timely action, which is essential for reaching the targeted 85% retention rate.

291 → 200
At-Risk Students: Current to Target

4. Progress Toward Strategic Goals

The dashboard tracks progress toward HSU's strategic goals:

  • Retention Rate: Current 70.9% → Target 85% (14.1% improvement needed)
  • At-Risk Student Count: Current 291 (29.1%) → Target 200 (20%) (91 students to support)
  • Average GPA: Current 2.77 → Target 2.95 (0.18 point improvement)
  • Intervention Response Rate: New Metric → Target 90% Compliance
